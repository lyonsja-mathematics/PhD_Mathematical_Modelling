This repository contains the final version of the programming scripts I wrote for my PhD thesis on *Mathematical Modelling of Hybrid Photonic Structures for Holographic Sensors*. My research was funded by the Irish Research Council (GOIPG/2021/214).

The motivation for this research project was as follows:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Optical properties of holographic gratings are sensitive to external stimulus and can be exploited for the purpose of environmental sensing.
        \item Hybrid photopolymers are a strong candidate as recording media for holographic gratings because they offer a wide dynamic range and good selectivity.
        \item A mathematical framework modelling the formation and operation of a holographic sensor is needed to optimize the design.
    \end{itemize}

The successful completion of this research was characterized by the ability to answer the following research questions.
    \begin{enumerate}
		\item How can we use the ...
		\begin{enumerate}
			\item host photopolymer material properties (monomer, binder matrix, dye, etc.)
            \item recording conditions (recording intensity, spatial frequency, etc.)
            \item nanoparticle properties (initial concentration, refractive index, etc.)
		\end{enumerate}
		in order to control the final grating in a hybrid polymer system and hence optimize the functionality of a holographic sensor? 
		
  \item Can the theoretical model predict IEO experimental results:
		\begin{enumerate}
		    \item Significantly increased dynamic range over conventional photopolymer media.
            \item Non-linear response of refractive index modulation to increased doping.
            \item Photopolymerization-induced shrinkage is significantly reduced by the addition of zeolite nanoparticles.
            \item Increased shrinkage at high spatial frequencies.
		\end{enumerate}
	\end{enumerate}
